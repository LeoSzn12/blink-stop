How to play

Blink Stop watches your eyes through the camera. Every game is about when you blink.

SET UP IN 10 SECONDS

  1. 1 Hold the phone at eye level, about an arm's length away.
  2. 2 Face a light or a window, not a bright light behind you.
  3. 3 Keep your eyes open normally while the bar fills.
  4. 4 The first time, blink once when asked. The eye light shows the game saw it.

THE GAMES

🎈

Balloon Pop

Blink when a balloon is in the green zone to pop it. A miss costs a life, and you have 3. It speeds up as you go, and every 50 pops the green zone shrinks. Pop a fast golden balloon for 5 points, or a holiday balloon for an extra life.

📱

Pass the Phone

2 to 8 players take turns on one phone. Pick Balloon Pop or Don't Blink. The top two meet in a Stare-Down final. The champion gets the crown, last place gets a dare.

👁️

Don't Blink

Keep your eyes open as long as you can. The screen gets wilder the longer you last.

🎯

Precision

Classic: blink right at 5, 10 or 15 seconds. The clock hides after 3, so count in your head. Gas Pump: blink to stop the pump at exactly $10.00. It speeds up after $8.

⚡

Flash

Wait for green, then blink as fast as you can. Your fastest of 3 tries counts.

🎵

Blink Beat

Blink as each note hits the line, in time with the beat. Turn the sound on.

👀

Stare-Down

Two players, cheek to cheek. First to blink loses.

😂

Jokes

The phone tells jokes while you try not to blink.

SHARING

Photos, clips and challenges

After a round, tap Share. Share Photo sends a framed photo with your result. Share Clip sends a few-second video of the moment you blinked. Share Challenge sends a link so a friend can try to beat you in the same game.

Photos and clips are made on your phone and only leave it when you share them.

Holiday skins

Pick a look before each game: a pilgrim hat for Thanksgiving, a Santa hat for Christmas, or a party hat. Skins only change what's drawn, never how blinks are seen.

HELP

It doesn't notice my blinks

Turn on a light in front of you and hold the phone at eye level. Glasses with strong glare can hide your eyes, so tilt them or try without. Your blink check is saved on this phone; to redo it, use Clear Local Data on the Support page.

Face not found, or the game won't start

Allow camera access, move a little closer, and add light. If eye tracking stalls, tap Restart camera. Home leaves setup at any time.

The camera is busy

Close other apps that use the camera (FaceTime, Zoom) and try again.

PLAY NOW

Blink normally between rounds and stop if your eyes feel uncomfortable. Ages 13+.
