How To Play Blink Stop

Game Modes

ENDURANCE

Who can keep their eyes open longest? Survive as long as possible without blinking. The longer you last, the more intense it gets with visual effects and faster audio!

PRECISION

Tap Precision to play with a 10 second target, or tap Change target time to pick 5, 10 or 15 seconds. Try to blink right on the target. The closer you get, the better your score.

30 SECONDS (under More ways to play)

Try to last 30 seconds without blinking. Take a break if your eyes feel uncomfortable.

DAILY CHALLENGE

Every day presents a new modifier condition, such as inverted colors, faster anxiety heartbeats, or screen glitching. Survive as long as you can under extra pressure.

SURPRISE MODE

Opt into a 30-second round with one sudden illustrated face at an unpredictable moment. Sound starts off and can be enabled before the round. There are no flashing effects. Blink whenever you need to; Menu ends the round.

Tips for Success

  • Stare at the center of the screen
  • Keep your eyes comfortably open while calibration samples your open-eye baseline
  • Good, even lighting on your face significantly helps with detection
  • Sit 1-2 feet away from your camera and ensure it is at eye-level

Troubleshooting Camera Issues

Game won't start?
Allow camera access. Calibration progress follows clear eye readings rather than restarting every three seconds. Face a light and hold your phone at eye level with your eyes comfortably open. If tracking cannot get ready within 15 seconds, the app returns to the menu with help. If eye tracking stalls, use Restart camera. You can leave calibration at any time with Back to menu.

Face not detected?
Improve lighting, move closer to the camera, and remove heavy glasses if possible.

Camera being used by another app?
Close other apps (Zoom, FaceTime, etc.) and try again.

Challenge a friend

After a valid round, share a portrait card or challenge caption. Your friend pastes the code under Have a friend's code? to play the same mode and target. Friend’s turn lets another player try on this phone. Precision compares timing error: lower is better. Other modes compare survival: higher is better, with a tie at the 30-second limit. Surprise timing varies and warns each player before starting. Daily has no friend code because modifiers depend on local date. Cards contain no camera photos; Share Photo is a separate choice. Blink normally and take breaks.

Challenge links and blink clips

After a round, Share Challenge sends a link like "I kept my eyes open for 14.00s as a turkey 🦃 Can you beat me?". Your friend taps it and sees the time to beat, in the same holiday skin, with one button to play. The time to beat stays on screen during their round. After a blink, Blink Stop also makes a few-second clip of the moment you blinked, hat and all. Tap Share Clip to post it to Reels, TikTok or the family chat. The clip is made on your phone and only leaves it when you share it.

Blink Olympics: Balloon Pop

Balloons float up past a glowing zone. Keep your eyes open, then blink when a balloon is inside the zone to pop it. Blinking with nothing in the zone, or letting a balloon float away, is a miss. Three misses and the round ends. Balloons get faster as you go, and they match your holiday skin.

Blink Olympics: Stare-down

Two players sit side by side, cheek to cheek, with a light in front of them and the phone at eye level. Once both faces are found, keep your eyes open: the first to blink loses and gets a dare. Blink together and it's a dead heat. If a face leaves the frame, the round doesn't count, so tap Rematch.

Blink Olympics: Pass the phone

Type 2 to 8 names, then pass the phone. Everyone takes one Endurance turn. The two longest stares meet in a head-to-head final (or skip it and the top stare wins). The champion gets a crown, last place gets a dare, and Share Reel puts everyone's caught-blinking photos in one picture. Names and photos stay on this phone and disappear when the party ends.

Blink Olympics: Distraction

The phone tells jokes while you stare, and friends can join in. Your time saves to its own leaderboard and can be shared as a challenge.

Holiday and party skins

Pick a skin on the menu: Thanksgiving puts a pilgrim hat and turkey feathers on your head, Christmas adds a Santa hat and snow, and Party adds a party hat and confetti. The hat follows your face on screen while you play. After a round, Share Photo sends a framed photo with your result, so the moment you blinked is ready for the family group chat. November starts on Thanksgiving and December on Christmas; your pick is remembered for the rest of the month. Skins only change what is drawn on your screen and in your photo, never how blinks are detected.
