Privacy Policy

Blink Stop is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data.

Camera Access

Blink Stop requires camera access to detect eye blinks using facial landmark detection, powered by MediaPipe.

Data Processing

All camera data is processed locally on your device in your web browser. We do not:

  • Upload or transmit camera footage to any server
  • Store camera images or video recordings
  • Share your camera data with third parties
  • Collect personally identifiable information

Local Storage

We use browser LocalStorage to save:

  • Leaderboard scores (name and time)
  • Unlocked themes and settings

This data remains on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use MediaPipe Face Mesh (Google) for facial landmark detection. The library runs entirely in your browser.

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
