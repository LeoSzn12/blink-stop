Privacy Policy

Blink Stop is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data.

Camera Access

Blink Stop requires camera access to detect eye blinks using facial landmark detection, powered by MediaPipe.

Data Processing

Camera frames are processed on your device by the packaged game. In this candidate we do not:

  • Upload or transmit camera footage to any server
  • Automatically save camera images or video recordings (a temporary selfie preview is created after a round; you can choose to save it)
  • Share your camera data with third parties
  • Send names or scores to a server in this version

Local Storage

We use browser LocalStorage to save:

  • Local scores (name and time)
  • Unlocked theme preference

Your local score and name remain on your device and can be cleared by clearing site/app data. Sharing a score sends only result text (no game link) through your device's share sheet or clipboard; saving a selfie writes an image only when you request it. The save destination depends on your device and browser.

Third-Party Services

We package MediaPipe Face Mesh (Google) for on-device facial landmark detection. No advertising or analytics SDK is enabled in this version. Pages use system fonts; they do not request remote font services. The game may still use the network to load its own assets and policies when visited online.
