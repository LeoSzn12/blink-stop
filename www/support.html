Support and Local Data

Blink Stop is operated by Nova Acquisitions LLC, California, USA. For support and privacy requests, email novaacquisitionsllc@gmail.com. Include your device model, OS/app version, game mode, and a description of the issue. Do not include sensitive photos.

Camera troubleshooting

Allow camera access in your browser or device settings, close other apps using the camera, and retry. Use ordinary lighting and center your face during calibration. If detection is uncomfortable or unreliable, stop playing. Camera images are processed on the device.

Saving and sharing

On iOS and Android, Save Selfie opens the system share sheet. Choose an available save destination or another app. On the website it downloads a JPEG. Share Score exports result text. A cancellation does not mean a file was saved. Copies you save elsewhere must be deleted in those destinations.

Clear local data

This removes Blink Stop's stored Classic, Precision, Endurance, Daily, and Surprise scores and theme preference on this installation, and attempts to remove its cached native selfie export. Exported copies and operating-system backups are not removed. Clearing your browser's site data or the app's data removes cached web assets too.

Surprise and bonus demo

Surprise Mode is an optional 30-second round with a sudden illustrated face. Sound is off by default. A separate bonus-theme demo uses a five-second placeholder after gameplay or from the menu, with the camera off. It makes no advertiser requests and earns no revenue. The amber demo theme lasts only until reload.

Version 1

The game is free, for a general audience age 13+, with no account, ads, analytics SDK, or in-app purchases. You can revoke camera permission in device settings. Take breaks and stop if your eyes become uncomfortable.
