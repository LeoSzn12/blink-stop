How To Play Blink Stop

Game Modes

CLASSIC MODE

Survive as long as possible without blinking. The longer you last, the more intense it gets with visual effects and faster audio!

PRECISION MODE

Choose a target time (5s, 10s, or 15s). Try to blink at the exact moment! Lower difference = better score.

ENDURANCE MODE

Try to last 30 seconds without blinking. Take a break if your eyes feel uncomfortable.

DAILY CHALLENGE

Every day presents a new modifier condition, such as inverted colors, faster anxiety heartbeats, or screen glitching. Survive as long as you can under extra pressure.

SURPRISE MODE

Opt into a 30-second round with one sudden illustrated face at an unpredictable moment. Sound starts off and can be enabled before the round. There are no flashing effects. Blink whenever you need to; Menu ends the round.

BONUS THEME DEMO

Preview a five-second placeholder between attempts, with the camera off. Completing it lets you apply an amber theme until reload; Skip leaves normal gameplay available. This prototype has no live advertising, advertiser requests, or ad revenue.

Tips for Success

  • Stare at the center of the screen
  • Keep your eyes comfortably open while calibration samples your open-eye baseline
  • Good, even lighting on your face significantly helps with detection
  • Sit 1-2 feet away from your camera and ensure it is at eye-level

Troubleshooting Camera Issues

Game won't start?
Grant camera permission in browser settings and refresh the page.

Face not detected?
Improve lighting, move closer to the camera, and remove heavy glasses if possible.

Camera being used by another app?
Close other apps (Zoom, FaceTime, etc.) and try again.
